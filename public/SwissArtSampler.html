OWAE Studio — Quick Help

Pads: Drop audio. Right-click for menu (Rename, Reverse, Normalize, Trim, Lock, Export, Clear). ⌘/Ctrl+drag to duplicate, drag to swap. Wheel=Gain, Alt+Wheel=Pitch.

Keys: Q–I / A–K trigger pads. 1–8 toggle clips. Space: Play/Stop. M: Metronome. ⌘/Ctrl+Z: Undo. ⌘/Ctrl+C/V: Copy/Paste pad.

Chop: Open Sampler → Chop; the 16 pads become slice cue points for the selected sample.

Capture: Jam on pads, press Capture to auto-detect BPM & quantize into the next clip.

Visuals: Pick a distinct 2D preset or enable WebGL scenes. Use Shuffle for a new seed. Save & load custom visuals.

Waveform: Click to set loop start, Shift+Click to set end. Drag to fine-adjust.

MIDI: Toggle MIDI Learn, hit a pad, then press a controller key to map.

Shortcuts: H Help, F Fullscreen, L WebGL, C Camera, R Master Rec, P Poster, O Export, I Import.
